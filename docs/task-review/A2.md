# A2 touch targets

Shared foundation sets mobile/coarse controls to at least 44px, desktop/fine targets to at least 24px, and includes checkbox/switch labels and disclosure headers. Plot points remain alternative visual selection with the keyboard-accessible candidate list; chart gestures are not stock navigation gestures. Inline paragraph links retain their text semantics.

Acceptance pending the all-route rendered bounds checker in Design Acceptance. Any remaining small target from a third-party component or portal is a failure to fix, not an exemption inferred from appearance.
