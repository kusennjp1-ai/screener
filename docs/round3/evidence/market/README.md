# Market-environment audit (round 3 side audit)

Read-only audit of the brief's market environment (市場 tab, Today's 地合い tile and the guide) against Minervini's and O'Neil's published practice.

- `market-plan.md`: the plan (Japanese). It has the owner summary, a 39-indicator mapping, MARKET track items M0–M10, later-round items L1–L13 and owner decisions OD-M1–OD-M11.
- `framework-mark.json` and `framework-william.json`: the sourced checklists (MM-01..21, W01..18).
- `minervini-trader/`, `oneil/` and `quant/`: persona audits. Their skeptic verifications are in `m1-*`, `m2-*` and `m3-*`.
- `completeness-critic/`: the gaps the personas missed.
- `inventory/`: the app inventory and renders. Large logs and the study release data are left out; they are reproducible from the cited runs.

Labels: 実測 = measured or read from code or logs; 出典 = the cited source says so; 推論 = replay, proxy or derivation; 未検証 = not obtained.
